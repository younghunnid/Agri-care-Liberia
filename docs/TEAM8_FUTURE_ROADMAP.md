# Team 8 — Post-Hackathon Future Roadmap
**Project:** **Agricare Liberia** (Inclusive Agricultural e-Advisory Platform)  
**Guiding Principle:** Build farmer and Ministry trust through agronomy validation, transparent simulation labels, and low-bandwidth rural reliability.

---

## Priority 1 — Immediate Post-Hackathon Foundations (Months 1–3)
1. **Agronomist & Veterinary Validation:** Partner with Ministry of Agriculture (MOA) extension specialists and CARI agronomists to review and certify every guide across our **9 Crops**, **6 Livestock & Aquaculture categories**, **Soil Advisory**, and **Pests & Diseases** modules.
2. **Bassa, Mano & Kpelle Community Voice Sprints:** Replace every **`Recording Coming Soon`** placeholder in `/language-lab` with consented, bilingual-verified native-speaker recordings (30–45 seconds each) for staple crops, poultry, fish ponds, and rainfall preparation.
3. **Real Telephone IVR & Toll-Free Integration:** Connect `/phone-simulator` to a Liberian mobile carrier shortcode or voice IVR gateway (Orange Liberia / Lonestar Cell MTN) so farmers on basic button phones receive real voice guidance without mobile data.
4. **Live County Weather Alerts:** Connect `/weather` from sample seasonal profiles to verified Liberia Meteorological Service / regional satellite rainfall feeds and SMS/voice risk alerts.

## Priority 2 — Ecosystem & County Field Pilot (Months 3–9)
5. **Verified Extension & Veterinary Triage Desk:** Transition the Simulated Expert Dashboard (`/experts`) into active county extension and veterinary officer queues with photo and voice-note response SLAs.
6. **Marketplace Verification & County Price Monitoring:** Add community verification badges for **Agricultural Suppliers** (seeds, cured compost, tools) and weekly enumerator-verified county market price bands (Gbarnga, Ganta, Red Light, Buchanan, Voinjama).
7. **Live Ministry Food Security Pipeline:** Replace synthetic dashboard series in `/ministry` with anonymized, consented real-time telemetry from registered farmer questions, pest reports, and county harvest listings.
8. **Offline MP3 / Opus Audio Bundles:** Allow PWA users to cache compact local-language audio packs alongside text guides (`/offline`) for zero-internet farm playback.

## Priority 3 — Long-Term Research & Ecosystem Scale (Months 9–18)
9. **AI Speech Recognition for Liberian Languages:** Train and benchmark open-weights ASR/intent models on Team 8's consented Bassa, Mano, and Kpelle audio corpus before removing experimental caveats.
10. **Validated Crop & Livestock Photo Triage:** Evaluate Liberia-calibrated computer vision models under agronomist oversight while maintaining mandatory non-diagnostic warnings.
11. **Cooperative Aggregation, Feeder-Road Transport & Farm Records:** Support rural cooperative harvest pooling, shared transport scheduling, voice-assisted farm record keeping, and vetted agricultural loan/grant eligibility checklists (linking transparently to `/coming-soon`).

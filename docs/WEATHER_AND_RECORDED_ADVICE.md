# Live weather, recorded rice advice and the rice question

## 1. Live weather (Open-Meteo)
**Provider:** Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/ (`/v1/forecast`). Fetched server-side by `server/weather.ts`; the browser never contacts the provider.

**Terms checked on 2026-11-21 (open-meteo.com/en/terms, /en/licence, /en/docs):**
- The free API is for **non-commercial use only**. Examples listed as non-commercial: private or non-profit sites and apps without subscriptions or advertising, education, public research. Commercial examples: subscriptions, display advertising, commercial products. **Agricare Liberia must stay non-commercial to use the free API** (no ads, no paid tiers). If the operator is a company, or this changes, get written confirmation from Open-Meteo or buy a plan (a paid plan uses an `apikey` parameter that this code does not yet send). This is a decision for the project owner.
- Free limits: 600 calls/minute, 5,000/hour, 10,000/day (and about 300,000/month), counted per IP. Our server shares ONE cached answer per place per 30 minutes, so 18 places cost at most 864 calls/day; device positions add one per distinct rounded position per 30 minutes. A per-visitor limit of 120 requests/hour protects the quota.
- **Attribution (CC BY 4.0):** a link "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com" to https://open-meteo.com/ next to every place the data is shown, plus the licence link. Done in `LiveWeatherPanel` (and in every API response under `report.source`).
- No warranty: the provider does not guarantee accuracy or availability. The app says forecasts can be wrong.
- Not verified: whether Hostinger allows outbound HTTPS to `api.open-meteo.com` (it normally does). If not, the weather says it cannot be loaded; nothing is invented.

**API (public, read-only):** `GET /api/v1/weather/locations`; `GET /api/v1/weather?location=<id>` or `?lat=&lon=` (Liberia only; rounded before use). Errors: 400 `INVALID_LOCATION`, 429, 503 `WEATHER_UNAVAILABLE`. Only a listed place or validated Liberian coordinates reach the provider; extra parameters are refused.
**Server behaviour:** 6 s timeout; the provider answer is checked field by field; a successful answer is cached 30 minutes; if the provider fails a copy up to 12 hours old is returned with `stale: true` and its ORIGINAL time; with no copy: 503. `WEATHER_API_BASE` exists only to point tests at a local stand-in (must be https in production).
**Places (18, county seat or main town first; positions approximate to about 5 km):** Tubmanburg (Bomi), Gbarnga (Bong), Bopolu (Gbarpolu), Buchanan (Grand Bassa), Robertsport (Grand Cape Mount), Zwedru (Grand Gedeh), Barclayville (Grand Kru), Voinjama and Zorzor (Lofa), Kakata (Margibi), Harper (Maryland), Monrovia and Paynesville (Montserrado), Sanniquellie and Ganta (Nimba), Cestos City (River Cess), Fish Town (River Gee), Greenville (Sinoe). Device location: only after the person taps "Use my location" and the browser permission prompt is accepted; rounded to 0.1 degree (about 11 km) on the phone and to 0.01 on the server; refused outside Liberia.
**Offline:** the last good forecast per place is saved on the phone (IndexedDB `apiCache`, key `liveweather:<place>`) with the time the provider data was retrieved. Offline or after an error it is shown as "Offline: saved forecast, may be old" / "Not live", with its age; days that have passed are hidden; reminders appear only for a live or recent (under 6 hours) forecast; with nothing saved no numbers are shown.
**Reminders** (`src/lib/weather/advice.ts`): heavy rain (check drainage), several wet days, little rain, strong wind, heat, thunderstorm. Worded as things the forecast shows and things you may want to check; always followed by "A forecast can be wrong. These are not expert-approved instructions".
**Where:** Farmer Today (compact card), `/weather` (full forecast with the older sample county profiles below, labelled "not a forecast"), USSD option 9.
**Privacy:** the chosen place or the rounded device position is sent to our server and then to Open-Meteo to get the forecast; the visitor's address, cookies and identity are not sent to the provider and nothing is stored on the server (only the cached forecast). `/privacy` mentions it.

## 2. The recording `vie.m4a`
- Original kept unchanged in the project root. Byte-identical copy served from `public/audio/rice/rice-recorded-advice.m4a` (SHA-256 `b29406bf989407d8c61c89c29911a9d06ff4f0902ed60b3c8d35c6b7f8c5abd4`, 1,082,973 bytes).
- Technical check (macOS `afinfo`, a full decode, a signal analysis, and playback in Chromium and the WebKit test runner): AAC-LC in an MP4 (.m4a) container, mono, 48 kHz, about 63 kbit/s, **130.18 seconds (2:10)**, no embedded title/artist/language tags; continuous speech-like signal (88% of half-second windows active, longest quiet stretch 3 s; it peaks at full scale, so it may be slightly clipped).
- **Language and content: Vai, rice yellow-leaves question: confirmed by the project owner on 2026-11-21.** No transcript exists and no independent Vai speaker or agricultural expert has checked it, so the screens say "Vai" and "confirmed by the project owner" and make no claim about what exactly it says. Its length (2 min 10 s) is longer than the one-sentence English answer.
- It is attached to the yellow-leaves card (`RECORDING_MATCHES_YELLOW_LEAVES = true`, `RICE_RECORDING.language = 'Vai'`) with the button **"Listen in Vai"** (then Pause / Continue / Replay / Play again). The English question and answer are unchanged and the review status stays **"Not reviewed"** until a qualified agricultural expert approves the advice. `/crops/rice` shows the one card (one player per page) plus a short "About the Vai recording" note.
- Player (`RecordedAdvicePlayer`): Play / Pause / Replay, never autoplays, plays the file as recorded (no generated speech), loads the file first and plays it from a Blob URL so iPhone Safari does not need byte-range support from the service worker, and calls `play()` straight from the tap. Low-data mode waits for a tap to load the 1.1 MB.
- Offline: the file is in the service worker's precache (the `m4a` pattern), versioned by the normal update mechanism. The player shows whether it is saved on the phone.

## 3. The rice question
Crop Rice. Question "My rice leaves are turning yellow. What could be the problem?" Answer "Yellow leaves may be caused by pests, diseases, lack of nutrients, or too much water. Check the plants carefully and contact an agricultural extension officer for proper advice." Data: `src/data/riceAdvice.ts`; card `RiceAdviceCard`; reachable from Knowledge & Guides ("Farmer questions by crop", Rice) and from the Rice crop page. Source: "Question and answer text supplied by the Agricare Liberia project team"; status "Not reviewed. No agronomist or Ministry of Agriculture review has been recorded." The card states the text is part of the app and opens offline.
